# The Tourney Pavilion of Azaria

**Type:** Feudal tournament grounds in a Shard of Azar
**Hook:** The 6-node starter's hub (`node_01_court`)

The tourney encampment: banners of the Nine Shards, heralds, betting rings, the Marshal's Altar for swearing the Vows, and the High Circle for the grand duel. Both the magic-feudal lists and the Seven Stars blademaster's challenge converge here.

```

```
