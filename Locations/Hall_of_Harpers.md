# The Hall of Harpers — Ikaris Gate Nexus

**Type:** The College of Harpers' hall — the World Gate nexus of Ikaris
**Hook:** The 6-node starter's lore node (`node_04_harpers_college`)

The hall where the Master Harper's emissaries track the gates — the Aradia portals in every Seven Stars city, the sealed Bazaroth Keys, and the waylines to Earth and the Inner Worlds. Fenlaeth guards an Aradia gate nearby; Mira reads the calibration.

**Mechanics:** A `lore` node with a `Sixth Sense (Gates)` reader — reach it, and the party learns the Cosmic Web is real on Ikaris, and which wayline the tourney is fighting over.
