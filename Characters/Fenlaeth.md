# Fenlaeth

**Race:** Dark Elf (arrived from an Outer World connected to Ikaris before its gate was destroyed — Ch.3 p.39)
**Faction:** Sylvan Exiles
**Role:** Guardian — guards a gate to Aradia
**Age:** unknown (centuries)

## Appearance

Slim, pale, dark-adapted eyes. A defensive ward hums around her, and she wears the cloak that keeps the sun off. Guard duty has made her quiet and watchful.

## Personality

Insular, precise, patient. Guards an Aradia gate in the shadow of the Harper Hall — the Sylvan Exiles' last honest trust. The sun is her only enemy she cannot outfight.

## Background

Dark Elf of the Outer World exiles, landed on Ikaris before her home gate was destroyed. The Sylvan Exiles found the College of Harpers and, in exchange for sanctuary, took up the ward of the Aradia portal. Fenlaeth is the current keeper — the one who keeps the angels' gate shut when it should be, and open when it must be.

## Abilities

Resilient (Ageing, Poisons), Supersense (Dark Vision) [racial]. Control Environment (Darkness), Healing. Blind Fighting, Deflection.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Subterranean Isolation — the guard has made her a stranger to the light.
- **Sixth Guard:** Sunlight Burnout — sun exposed without cloak → blinded and ACV-penalized.
- **Levers:** Shadow Weave / Aradia Ward / Piercing Starlight
