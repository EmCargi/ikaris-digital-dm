# Archmage Torvane

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** The Tourney Bench — High Council of Azar
**Role:** Tourney judge, Vow-enforcer
**Age:** 60s

## Appearance

Bald, unbending, robed in the Robes of Order (AR 5). Eyes that weigh oaths. A writ of censure is never far from hand.

## Personality

Dogmatic, precise, fair to the letter of the Vows. Judges the Midsummer Tourney with total commitment to the code — magic must not harm the innocent, and protocol is the only law. His rigidity is his armor and his cage.

## Background

Archmage of a Shard's high bench for decades. Trained Aerin; judged dozens of tourneys; personally brokered the College of Harpers' seat at the gate nexus. The Vows are not a preference — they are the whole of the law.

## Abilities

Mind Shield, Dispel/Nullify Magic. Judge Opponent. Law (Feudal) 3, Occult 3. Robes of Order (AR 5).

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Dogmatic Rigidity — the letter of the Vows blanks their spirit.
- **Sixth Guard:** Lexicon Breakdown — forbidden planar magic cast → paralyzed by theological contradiction.
- **Levers:** Writ of Censure / Null Pulse / Feudal Injunction
