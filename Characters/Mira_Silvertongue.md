# Mira Silvertongue

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** College of Harpers
**Role:** Harper — knows the gates, neutral broker of the tourney
**Age:** 34

## Appearance

Compact, quick-fingered, a harp on her back and a Charter in her sleeve. Moves through the tournament like she owns the crowd — because she does.

## Personality

Wry, diplomatic, unclaimable. A Harper's ear is a passport to every court and every gate. Neutrality is her power; the moment she's forced to take a side, she loses the ear and the immunity.

## Background

Trained at the College, advised a Legate's court, and learned the deeper charge: the Master Harper is the World Gatekeeper, and the College tracks every gate on Ikaris. She reads the Aradia portals and the Bazaroth Keys the way other harpers read music.

## Abilities

Sixth Sense (World Gates), Telepathy. Concealment, Portable Armoury. Social 3, Street 2, Performing Arts 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Diplomatic Overreach — the song that tries to please everyone.
- **Sixth Guard:** Concordat Forfeit — forced to take an overt factional side → loses diplomatic immunity and Harper ear.
- **Levers:** Silver Bargain / Tune of Dissension / Gatekeeper's Pass
