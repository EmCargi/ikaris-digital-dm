# Ronan the Oathless

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** The Forsaken Lists — oath-broken warlock
**Role:** Moral thrall — redeem him or use him
**Age:** 41

## Appearance

Broken staff at his belt, oathbreaker runes scorched into his forearms, heavy greatsword slung across his back. Walks like a man who has already been condemned and keeps walking anyway.

## Personality

Spite-driven, weary, capable of desperate loyalty. Forswore his Vows — and the runes prove it. He can be a monster or a Robin Hood, depending entirely on whether anyone gives him a reason to be the latter.

## Background

A sworn sorcerer who saw his archmage turn evil and his Legate refuse to listen. He broke his Vows to stop them — the staff snapped, the runes burned in, and the Forsaken Lists took him. At the tourney, he's entered the lists as a mercenary: the grand duel's dark horse, and the party's choice.

## Abilities

Heavy Greatsword, Weapon: Nether Flare. Brutal, Lethal Blow. Military 2, Street 2. Defect: Marked (Oathbreaker runes).

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Spite-Driven Leverage — anger is the fuel, and it burns both ways.
- **Sixth Guard:** Soul Debt Foreclosure — adversary invokes the broken oath → wracked by phantom branding.
- **Levers:** Desperate Cleave / Nether Lash / Defiant Roar
