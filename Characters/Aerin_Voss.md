# Aerin Voss

**Race:** Mageborn (gift from the Weaver — detected in infancy)
**Faction:** High Council of Azar
**Role:** Sorcerer — newly staffed, sworn to a Shard's Legate
**Age:** 24

## Appearance

Young, sharp-eyed, staff newly won. Arcane focus faintly glowing at the haft. Wears the slate robe of a sworn sorcerer with the Vows' sigil stitched at the collar.

## Personality

Brilliant, ambitious, proud. Swore the Vows eagerly — believes the Gift is a duty to protect the weak. But hubris runs hot in a new staff; overchannelling is a habit he hasn't broken.

## Background

Raised in the High Council's academy, adopted as heir-apprentice by an archmage who saw his Gift in infancy. Won his staff at a minor tourney, swore the Vows at the Marshal's Altar, and was posted to the Midsummer Tourney to prove himself before the Legates.

## Abilities

Force Field, Weapon: Arcane Bolt (Range). Precise Aim, Lightning Reflexes. Occult 2, Academic 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Hubristic Channelling — the Gift is a weapon he over-commits.
- **Sixth Guard:** Mana Hemorrhage — depleted to 0 EP → arcane feedback burns physical health.
- **Levers:** Ward Stance / Flame Lance / Spire Aegis
