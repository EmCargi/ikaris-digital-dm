# Kito Hyou

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Seven Stars Schools
**Role:** Blademaster — somatic spellcasting, the wildcard
**Age:** 30

## Appearance

Lean, callused, twin wakizashi and a katana worn in the Seven Stars style. Eyes steady as a held blade. The Shards call his fighting "magic" — he lets them.

## Personality

Disciplined, proud, exact. The Duelist's Pride is his whole art: every blade stroke is a prayer, every parry a vow. His cadence is sacred — break it, and he is broken.

## Background

Trained in a Seven Stars School — magic forbidden there, so weapon mastery became the somatic spellcasting the Shards treat with awe. Crossed the Dragon's Spine to test the magic-feudal lists at the Midsummer Tourney, where a blademaster's "magic" unsettles every sorcerer on the bench.

## Abilities

Two Weapons, Critical Strike, Lightning Reflexes. Adventuring 3, Military 2. Twin Wakizashi / Katana.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Duelist's Pride — the cadence is sacred; a flaw is a sin.
- **Sixth Guard:** Broken Cadence — parried on consecutive strikes → off-balance and stripped of defensive reactions.
- **Levers:** Flowing Form / Crescent Sever / Seven Stars Step
