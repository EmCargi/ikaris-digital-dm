# The High Council of Azar

**Ruler:** The Legates of the Nine Shards + their archmagi
**Stronghold:** The Shards of Azar (west coast)
**Doctrine:** Feudal magic, Vows of Azar, tournament as status

The High Council isn't a single throne — it's the loose compact of the nine Legates, each served by archmagi and sworn sorcerers. Magic is the ultimate weapon; the Vows forbid harming the innocent but not mage pride. Tournaments settle disputes and distribute prestige (and lent artefacts).

**Cast hook:** **Aerin Voss** (Mageborn Sorcerer, High Council of Azar, `[Oath: Sorcerer]`).
