# The College of Harpers

**Leader:** The Master Harper — the World Gatekeeper of Ikaris
**Stronghold:** Harper halls across the Shards + each Seven Stars city
**Doctrine:** Track the gates, advise Legates, keep the Cosmic Web's secrets

The College of Harpers traditionally appoints a learned harper to advise each Legate. Its deeper charge: **the Master Harper is the gatekeeper of Ikaris**, inheriting the position from the last Emperor's harpist. The College tracks the status of all gates — especially the Bazaroth Keys (to seal demons) and the Aradia gates (in every Seven Stars city).

**Cast hook:** **Mira Silvertongue** (College of Harpers, `[Oath: Harper]`) — knows the gates, the neutral broker of the tourney.
