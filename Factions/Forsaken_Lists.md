# The Forsaken Lists & Warlocks

**Type:** Oath-breakers — ex-sorcerers, expelled or self-forsaken
**Rite:** Staff broken, honor stripped, reviled (much like ronin)
**Fate:** Bandit leaders, wandering mercenaries, monster-killers, bounty-hunters

Some warlocks forswear for good reasons (an evil archmage, a Legate who won't listen) and lead bands of "good" outlaws — a magical Robin Hood. Some archmagi hire warlocks in war, though it's considered sinister. A warlock can swear new allegiance if an archmage or Legate pardons them, but it requires a great deed.

**Cast hook:** **Ronan the Oathless** (The Forsaken Lists, `[Oath: Warlock]`) — the moral-thrall slot; redeem him or use him.
