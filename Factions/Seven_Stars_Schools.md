# The Seven Stars Schools

**Type:** Fighting monks/nuns of the Seven Stars — spirit, exorcism, personal perfection
**Doctrine:** Magic forbidden (prophet Achmed); martial arts as spiritual focus
**Reputation:** Blademasters' fighting seen as *magical* — regarded with supernatural awe

The Schools train vows of poverty and obedience. Their powers are exotic: spirits, exorcism, and martial arts from focusing spiritual energy. Weapon mastery is a somatic spellcasting — a Seven Stars blademaster is the closest thing to a holy warrior the east has.

**Cast hook:** **Kito Hyou** (Seven Stars Schools, `[Oath: Blademaster]`) — the wildcard whose dueling culture clashes with the magic-feudal lists.
