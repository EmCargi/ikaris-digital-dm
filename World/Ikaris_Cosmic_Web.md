# Ikaris & The Cosmic Web

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 332–333 (Ikaris).

## The Gatekeeper of Ikaris

Ikaris has **seven waylines**: one to Earth, two to Inner Worlds, and four to the other Prime Worlds save Enid. The Empire of Azar knew of the Cosmic Web, and the **Emperor was the gatekeeper of Ikaris**. The position passed in secret to his harpist — today the gatekeeper is the **Master Harper**. The College of Harpers keeps track of the status of the gates.

## Gates & Factions

- Gates are scattered across the world. The gatekeeper struggles to locate the Keys to the **Bazaroth** gates to keep demons sealed.
- **Aradia** is active on Ikaris — the legendary angels arrived from Aradia to join the founders of the Seven Stars, and there are gates to Aradia in each of the seven cities.
- The **Seraphic Knights** are emissaries from Aradia sent to protect the descendants of the Seven Stars.

## Cast Hook

**Fenlaeth** (Dark Elf Guardian, Sylvan Exiles) guards a gate to Aradia; **Mira Silvertongue** (College of Harpers) tracks the portals. The tourney's lore node is the Harper Hall — the Ikaris World Gate nexus.
