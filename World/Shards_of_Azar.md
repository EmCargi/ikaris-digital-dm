# The Shards of Azar

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 330–331 (Ikaris).

## Overview

On Ikaris's west coast lie the Shards of Azar — successor states of the once-great Empire of Azar, which ruled the entire coast until it broke apart 300 years ago in a great civil war. The Shards are nine squabbling principalities, each ruled by a powerful **Legate** (absolute ruler, king-equivalent).

## Feudal Magic

The Shards mirror medieval European feudalism with one difference: **magic works, and a mage's fireball is the ultimate weapon** — not the mounted knight. Each Legate is served by several archmagi; each archmage's province is divided into mage-holds, each controlled by a sworn sorcerer who owes 40 days' service. Treachery is fomented by rival Legates.

## The Vows of Azar

The ancient Vows are a magical code all true sorcerers, archmagi, and Legates must adhere to: **forbid harming the innocent, encourage protecting the weak.** But mages are proud — they won't brook challenge from mundanes, and a sorcerer holds power of life and death over serfs.

**Warlocks** are oath-breakers: ex-sorcerers expelled or who forsook their vows, staff broken, reviled (much like ronin). Some lead bandit gangs; others wander as mercenaries, monster-killers, or bounty-hunters — occasionally for good reason.

## Tournaments

Archmagi and Legates hold **regular tournaments** — single duels, apprentice matches, grand duels of powers, monster-baiting contests, summoning competitions, and games for the wizard's spearmen. The prize is usually a valuable artefact lent until the next tourney. Tournaments are the currency of status and dispute resolution.

## Cast Hook

**Aerin Voss** (Mageborn Sorcerer, High Council of Azar), **Archmage Torvane** (the Tourney Bench), **Ronan the Oathless** (Forsaken Lists). The 6-node tourney module runs in one Shard's grounds.
