# The Anvil of God & Tophet

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 331 (Ikaris).

## The Anvil of God

A vast steppe and desert in Ikaris's centre, inhabited by tribes of beast nomads (the **Wyvern**, **Gryphon**, and **Manticore** tribes) who migrate around the edges and raid the settled nations. The Dragon's Spine acts as a barrier, limiting their ability to attack west.

## The Dragon's Spine

An immense mountain range bisecting the supercontinent east-to-west. Named for the winged **firedrakes** that dwell there, preying on migratory herds and occasionally traveling into the Shards or Seven Stars. It's the borderland between the north and south.

## Tophet

A massive underground labyrinth and ruined city near the eastern spur of the Dragon's Spine, designed as shelter against dragon raids. Once the capital of the **Magerealm of Tophet**, a rival to the Empire of Azar. The Archmage of Tophet controlled many monsters; when he was slain in a magical duel, the monsters escaped and overran the city.

## Cast Hook

Border threats for the tourney's monster-baiting contest — an escaped **Azari border drake** from the Dragon's Spine foothills (the sand-pit encounter).
