# The Land of Seven Stars

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 331–332 (Ikaris).

## Overview

Across the continent from the Shards of Azar lies the Land of Seven Stars, the Heavenly Coast. Legend: seven celestial angels founded seven villages that grew into seven cities. The land is temperate, culture a cross between Ancient Greece and medieval Japan. There are seven secret "undercities" and a legend of parallel shadowy demon clans.

## The Four Castes

- **"Heaven-born" lords** (the ruling warriors)
- **Their warriors**
- **The priests**
- **Commoners** (farmers, artisans, merchants)

## The Prophecy of Achmed

Magic is **forbidden** in the Seven Stars — the prophet Achmed taught that performing magic corrupts the soul; those who do will be reincarnated as rats or insects. Instead there are fighting monks and nuns with exotic powers: spirits, exorcism, personal perfection, and martial arts from focusing spiritual energy. All swear vows of poverty and obedience.

## The Blademasters

Weapon mastery is regarded as **inherently magical** — somatic spellcasting. True swordsmen like the blademasters of the Seven Stars are regarded with almost supernatural awe, their fighting prowess seen as magic. Western mages (warlocks, nomad witches) are feared and distrusted here.

## Cast Hook

**Kito Hyou** (Seven Stars Schools, Blademaster) — high Body/ACV, twin blades, "fighting seen as magical." His dueling culture clashes with the Shards' magic-feudal lists at the tourney.
